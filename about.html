About Sipho's Plumbing & Maintenance

Our History

Sipho's Plumbing & Maintenance was started in 2016 as a one-person operation serving a handful of streets in Johannesburg's northern suburbs. Word of mouth from satisfied homeowners has grown the business into a trusted local name, still built on the same principle: turn up on time, fix it properly the first time, and charge a fair price.

What began with a single toolbox and a bakkie has grown into a small operation handling everything from dripping taps to full geyser replacements — but the day-to-day approach hasn't changed: Sipho still personally assesses every emergency call-out.

Our Mission

To provide fast, honest, and fairly-priced plumbing services to Johannesburg homeowners and small businesses.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted local plumbing name in the northern suburbs, growing from a sole proprietor into a small, reliable team of qualified technicians.

Our Values

Honesty

Upfront pricing before work starts, and no hidden call-out surprises.

Reliability

If we say we'll be there at 2pm, we're there at 2pm.

Quality

Work done to code the first time, so you're not calling us back for the same problem.
