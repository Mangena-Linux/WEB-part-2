Our Services

From emergency repairs to planned maintenance, here is how we can help.

Plumbing Repairs

General plumbing repairs for homes and small businesses, including:

  • Burst and leaking pipe repairs
  • Blocked drain and toilet clearing
  • Tap and mixer repairs and replacements
  • Water pressure diagnosis

Typical response time: Same day for urgent leaks, within 48 hours for non-urgent repairs.

Geyser Services

Geyser problems rarely wait for a convenient time, so this is our most requested emergency service:

  • Geyser installation and replacement
  • Geyser repairs, including element and thermostat faults
  • Emergency call-outs for burst or leaking geysers
  • Compliance certificates on request

Typical response time: Emergency geyser call-outs are prioritised and usually attended to within hours.

General Maintenance

Ongoing maintenance plans to help homeowners and small commercial properties avoid costly emergencies:

  • Scheduled plumbing inspections
  • Preventative maintenance contracts
  • Small repairs bundled into a single planned visit

Maintenance plans: Quarterly and bi-annual inspection contracts available — ask for a quote tailored to your property.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge a call-out fee?

Our call-out fee is waived if you go ahead with the repair. If you decide not to proceed, a small call-out fee covers the technician's time and travel.

Are you available on weekends?

Yes — emergency call-outs (burst pipes, geyser failures) are available every day. Non-urgent bookings run Monday to Saturday.

Do you provide compliance certificates?

Yes, we issue plumbing compliance certificates for geyser installations and replacements on request, as required for property sales and insurance.

Not sure what you need?

Tell us what's going on and we'll recommend the right service.
